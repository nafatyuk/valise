Valise

A personal, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public.

A valise is the diplomatic bag: a sealed pouch carried between posts and never opened on the way.

Valise runs on its owner's own Mac. It lets the owner's AI coding assistants (such as Claude Code or Codex) search, read, and organize the owner's own mail, on the owner's request, through the mail apps already on that Mac (Mimestream and Outlook).

What it does with Google

It signs in to the owner's own Google accounts with the Gmail API scope gmail.modify and uses it only to read messages and labels and to change labels (for example archive, star, mark read, or file under a label). It does not send mail and cannot permanently delete mail.

Privacy policy

Valise is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with Mimestream, Microsoft, or Google.
